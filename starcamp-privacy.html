Privacy Policy — StarCamp

Last updated: 2026-08-21
App: StarCamp (StarCamp Signal Map)
Publisher: ZBENS
Bundle ID: com.zbens.starcampsignalmap

Overview

StarCamp helps digital nomads and vanlifers understand Starlink and cellular signal conditions at campsites (maps, community reports, optional AR sky-scan predictions). Core browsing works with local seed data. Optional cloud features use Supabase when configured.

Data we may collect

  • Account — email and optional username if you sign up (via Supabase Auth).
  • Contributions — signal reports you submit (speeds, latency, notes, campsite id).
  • Approximate location — only when you grant permission, used to rank nearby sites. We do not store continuous GPS trajectories.
  • On-device — AR sky-scan results and preferences stored locally (e.g. SharedPreferences / SQLite).
  • Map tiles — OpenStreetMap tiles may be cached on disk for offline viewing (non-iOS builds).

How we use data

  • Show campsite signal maps and community reports.
  • Improve meeting-fitness and obstruction predictions.
  • Sync offline-queued reports when you reconnect.

Sharing

  • Public campsite aggregates and reports may be visible to other users.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Infrastructure: Supabase (auth + database) when enabled. Push (Firebase) and crash reporting (Sentry) are optional and disabled until explicitly enabled.

Retention & deletion

  • You may delete your account from the app. That removes your profile and anonymizes or deletes linked reports where technically feasible.
  • Local scan history can be cleared on the device.

Your rights

Where applicable (e.g. GDPR), you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, or objection. Contact us via zbens.com/feedback.html or privacy@zbens.com.

Children

StarCamp is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will update the “Last updated” date on this page.
