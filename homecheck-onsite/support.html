HomeCheck Onsite — Support

On-site AR daylight & sensor inspection for home visits.

Quick tips

  • Sunlight: grant Camera + Motion; follow the skyline scan or quick annotate path.
  • Noise: grant Microphone; compare window open vs closed if testing insulation.
  • Reports: save locally; export PDF via the system share sheet.
  • Subscription: free tier allows a limited number of homes; yearly unlock is managed in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
