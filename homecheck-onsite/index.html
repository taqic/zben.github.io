Privacy Policy — HomeCheck Onsite

Last updated: 2026-09-07
App: HomeCheck Onsite (on-site AR property inspection)
Publisher: ZBENS
Bundle ID: com.zbens.HomeCheckOnsite

Summary

HomeCheck Onsite helps you measure daylight, noise, ceiling height, magnetic anomalies, and cross-breeze structure during a property visit. Inspection reports are stored on your device. We do not require a cloud account, and we do not upload property photos, microphone audio, or raw GPS tracks to ZBENS servers.

Data stored on your device

  • Inspection report drafts and history (JSON in app documents)
  • Optional magnetometer hard-iron calibration offsets
  • Subscription entitlement flags restored via Apple / Google billing
  • Language and in-app preferences

Uninstalling the app typically deletes this local data.

Permissions

  • Camera — AR skyline / sunshine scanning and optional inspection photos (processed on device)
  • Motion / sensors — leveling, pose assist, barometric height, magnetometer scan
  • Microphone — noise level and window insulation tests (audio is not uploaded)
  • Location (when in use) — solar-position math; optional weather correction

Third-party services

When you enable weather-related daylight correction, the app may send approximate coordinates to Open-Meteo (open meteorological API) to fetch diffuse/direct irradiance. That request does not include your name, Apple ID, or report contents. Open-Meteo’s own terms apply to that query.

In-app purchases / subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. ZBENS does not receive your payment card details.

Free tier & subscription

You may save a limited number of properties for free. An optional yearly subscription unlocks unlimited saved reports. Manage or cancel in Apple ID / Google Play subscription settings.

Sharing and export

PDF export and the system share sheet are under your control. We do not receive those shares unless you email them to us yourself.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

Contact

Questions: zbens.com/feedback or the publisher contact on the App Store product page. Support: homecheck-onsite/support
