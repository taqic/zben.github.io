Privacy Policy — Kitchen Sync

Last updated: 2025-11-10

Overview

Kitchen Sync is a recipe management app focused on privacy. The app follows a local‑first design: your recipes, notes, plans and images are stored on your device by default. We do not operate any backend that receives, tracks, or sells your personal data.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, share or sell personal data to our servers.

The app may process the following data locally on your device to provide core features:

  • Recipes, tags, collections, meal plans, shopping lists and pantry inventory (stored locally using SQLite/Hive).
  • Imported content from web links or images. OCR text recognition is performed on device using Google ML Kit.
  • Basic settings (theme, unit preferences, language).

Permissions

  • Camera / Photos: to import recipes from images and scan text (OCR). Images are kept locally unless you export or share.
  • Storage: to read/write recipe data and cached images locally.
  • Notifications (optional): for timers or reminders you enable.

No permission is used for purposes other than providing the corresponding feature.

Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties. When you choose to export or share a recipe, the content you select will be shared via the target app or channel you choose.

Data Retention & Deletion

All user data is stored on your device and remains until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app removes the locally stored data.

Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We use platform‑provided storage and follow best practices to minimize data exposure. Because we do not run cloud servers for this app, there is no remote profile of your data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

Contact

If you have any questions or requests (e.g., export assistance), contact: support@kitchensync.app

隐私政策（简体中文）

概述：Kitchen Sync 采用本地优先设计，食谱、批注、计划与图片默认保存在您的设备本地。我们不运营接收或出售个人数据的服务器。

收集与使用：应用不会向我们服务器上传您的个人数据。为提供核心功能，应用会在本地处理并保存食谱数据、从网页/图片导入的内容（OCR 在设备上完成）、以及偏好设置。

权限用途：相机/照片用于图片导入与 OCR；存储用于本地保存数据；通知仅在您启用计时/提醒时使用。权限仅用于对应功能。

共享与保留：我们不与第三方共享数据。仅当您主动导出或分享时，选中的内容才会通过您选择的渠道分享。卸载应用会删除本地数据。

儿童隐私：本应用并非面向 13 岁以下儿童。

安全：使用平台存储并遵循最小化数据暴露原则。由于没有云端账户，您的数据不会被我们集中存储。
