Privacy Policy — Cupdial

Last updated: 2026-08-11
App: Cupdial (Coffee Dial-in Journal)
Publisher: ZBENS

Summary

Cupdial is an offline-first coffee brewing journal. We do not require a cloud account, and we do not upload your brewing data to our servers by default.

Data stored on your device

The app may store locally on your device:

  • Coffee bean profiles and photos you add
  • Brewing records, ratings, flavor notes, and extraction steps
  • Equipment entries
  • App preferences (theme, language, units)

Data is stored using on-device storage (SQLite / SharedPreferences / app documents). Uninstalling the app typically deletes this data unless you previously exported a backup.

Permissions

Depending on platform, the app may request:

  • Camera / Photo library — only to attach coffee or brew photos you choose
  • Storage / files — only for export, import, or backup files you initiate

Permissions are used for the stated features and are not used for advertising tracking.

Sharing and export

If you use export, backup, or the system share sheet, files or text leave the app under your control (for example to Files, Messages, or email). We do not receive those shares.

Third-party services

The current free/open build does not require Firebase, analytics SDKs, or paid cloud APIs for core journaling. If a future optional Pro sync feature is enabled, this policy will be updated before collection begins.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information of children through the app.

Your choices

  • Change language, theme, and units in Settings
  • Export or delete local data in Settings
  • Revoke camera/photos permission in system Settings

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

For privacy questions about Cupdial, contact us via zbens.com/feedback or email the publisher listed on the App Store product page.
