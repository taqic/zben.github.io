Privacy Policy — Adventurer's Log

Last updated: August 14, 2026 · Bundle ID: com.zbens.adventurerslog

Adventurer's Log (“the App”, 冒险者手册) is a local-first TTRPG / D&D companion for character sheets, spell slots, session journals, and table tools. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Data We Collect

The App does not require a developer-operated account and does not sell personal data to advertising networks. The current release does not include mandatory analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Characters, spells/items, spell-slot state, journals, achievements, and settings are stored only on your device (SQLite / preferences).
  • Data is not automatically uploaded to servers operated by the developer.
  • Uninstalling the App may remove local data (per operating-system behavior).

3. Optional WebDAV Backup

  • If you configure WebDAV under Settings → Cloud sync and upload/download, the App stores your WebDAV URL, username, and password on device and sends a backup JSON to the server you specify (for example Nutstore).
  • That backup is under your control and subject to your storage provider’s terms. Prefer app-specific passwords when available.
  • Without WebDAV configuration, the App works fully offline.

4. Sharing & Exports

Exports (backup JSON, Markdown/PDF) may use the system share sheet or file picker. Content you choose to share is under your control and subject to the receiving app’s policies.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children’s data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised accordingly.
