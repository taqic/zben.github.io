Privacy Policy — HostReach

Last updated: August 11, 2026

HostReach (“the App”) is a network connectivity utility that lets you ping hostnames or IP addresses you choose. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Data We Collect

HostReach does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal information.

  • No accounts. The App does not require sign-in.
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs in the current release.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Ping history (hosts you tested and summary statistics) is saved only on your device using on-device preferences storage.
  • You can clear history in the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes local history.

3. Network Access

  • The App sends ICMP (or equivalent platform ping) probes to targets you enter.
  • On iOS, access to the local network may be requested so you can ping LAN devices (for example a router such as 192.168.1.1). You can deny this permission; probes to public Internet hosts may still work depending on the platform.
  • Probe targets and results are not uploaded by HostReach.

4. Third Parties

Sharing exported CSV uses the system share sheet. Content you choose to share is under your control and subject to the receiving app’s policies.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children’s data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised accordingly.
