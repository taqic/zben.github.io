Privacy Policy — Ink Rhythm

Last updated: 2026-08-07

Product: Ink Rhythm (墨韵) for Android / iOS

Overview

Ink Rhythm is a fountain-pen, ink, paper, and swatch collection app. This release is local-first and does not offer a public community, comments, or public UGC.

Data we process

On-device (default)

Pens, inks, paper, swatches, photos you add, color-search history, and settings are stored on your device and are not uploaded automatically.

Optional account & cloud sync

Only if you register/sign in and sync: email, display name, bio, avatar URL (if provided), credentials (password hashed server-side), and collection data you choose to sync.

Permissions

PermissionPurpose
CameraColor picking and photos of pens / inks / swatches
PhotosChoosing images; saving exports when used

Not collected in this release

  • No public feed or comments
  • No sale of personal data to ad networks
  • No required precise location

Deletion

  • Clear all on-device data: Settings → Privacy → Delete all local data
  • Delete account: Settings → Privacy → Delete account (signed-in)

Contact

Use in-app Settings → Contact support, or email matt@zbens.com.

隐私政策（简体中文）— 墨韵

生效日期：2026-08-07

墨韵是钢笔、墨水、纸张与试色收藏工具。数据默认保存在本机； 仅在你主动注册登录并使用云同步时，才会将账号与收藏备份至所连接的服务器。 当前上架版不提供公开社区、评论或 UGC。

权限：相机用于取色与藏品照片；相册用于选择图片与导出。

删除：设置 → 隐私 → 删除本机全部数据；登录后可注销账号删除云端数据。

联系：应用内「设置 → 联系支持」或 matt@zbens.com。
